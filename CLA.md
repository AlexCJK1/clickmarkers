Thank you for contributing. This agreement is short and it is the same for everyone. You sign it once,
by posting a comment on your first pull request (the CLA bot tells you the exact words). It covers every
contribution you make to Nightdesk after that.

licence to use it in Nightdesk, including the right to offer Nightdesk under other licences (for example
a commercial licence to a company that cannot use the AGPL). In return, the maintainer promises that any

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot comments on it. Reply with exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub name and the date in the `cla-signatures` branch of the repository.
If you sign on behalf of an organisation, say so in the same comment.
