# Agency template

The full example company from the demo: Larkspur Cleaning Co., a made-up window cleaner in Bristol, run
by 30 desks in seven departments (executive, marketing, leads, inbox, operations, finance, delivery), with
12 routines and three workflows. It shows what the frame can hold; most businesses start from the
five-desk company in `company/` and add desks as they need them.

To use it instead of the starter:

1. Copy `company.json`, `routines.json` and `workflows.json` into your office folder's `company/`
   (the folder `clickmarkers init` made), over the starter files.
2. Rewrite the business: the mission, the house rules, every desk's brief and the notes they read.
3. `npm run check` names any desk whose connector is not set up yet. Pause the routines you do not need
   under **Routines** on the page.

Sixteen of its desks run on Opus. That suits a demo of what the office can do; for daily use, put the
desks that only write or sort on Sonnet and watch the ledger.
