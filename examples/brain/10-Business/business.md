# The business

Larkspur Cleaning Co. is a small window and home cleaning business in Bristol, run by Sam with three
cleaners and one van. Most work is regular window rounds and one-off deep cleans. New customers find us
on Facebook and Instagram, send a message for a quote, and book a first clean.

Goal this season: 40 new regular customers by the end of March, at under £6 of ad spend per enquiry.
