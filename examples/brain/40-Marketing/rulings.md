# Rulings

1. Prices only from [[price-list]], always written "from £X".
2. No dates or time slots promised: the owner books the diary.
3. No faces, house numbers or car registrations in any picture ([[consent]]).
4. The only trust claims: "fully insured" and "DBS-checked cleaners". No superlatives.
5. Every post carries a tracked link with its own tag (fb01, fb02, ...).
6. British English, no em dashes.
