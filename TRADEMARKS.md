"Nightdesk" and the project's logo are trademarks of Alex Cojoaca (unregistered). They say where a
copy comes from, so people who use a copy called Nightdesk know what they are getting.

## You may, without asking

- Use, change and run Nightdesk under its own name for yourself or inside your own business.
- Say that your project is "based on Nightdesk", or that it "works with Nightdesk", in plain text.
- Write about Nightdesk, review it, teach it and link to it.
- Ship unmodified copies under the name, for example in a package or an install script.

## You may not, without written permission

- Call a modified version, a fork or a hosted service "Nightdesk", or a name that is easily confused
  with it (such as "Nightdesk Pro" or "NightDesk Cloud").
- Use the name or the logo in a way that suggests the author made, checked or endorses your version.
- Use the name in a company name, a product name, a domain name or an app store listing.

## Attribution is separate

Whatever you call your version, the licence's additional terms ask you to keep the line "Nightdesk by
Alex Cojoaca" and its link in the About section and the README (see `NOTICE`). Renaming does not
remove that.
