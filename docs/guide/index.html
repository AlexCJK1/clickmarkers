How to use your office

Clickmarkers, in ten minutes

Your office runs on your own computer at 127.0.0.1:4600. Desks do the work; you give jobs, read results and say yes or no to anything that goes out. The pictures are the demo office, a made-up window cleaner: run npm run demo:show to click round it yourself.

The main screen

Tap a number to see what that part does. Everything else opens from here and closes with Esc.

    Give the office a job

    1. Click the job bar at the bottom of the war room, or press /. Press M to speak it instead.
    2. Write it as you would to a person: design three posts on spring window cleaning, or tomorrow 9am chase the stuck quotes.
    3. Press Send. The office picks the right desk. To choose one yourself, use Who, or start the job with the desk's name: DESIGN: a post on gutter clearing.
    Later books it for a date and time. Big editor (Ctrl+Shift+E) gives a large box for a long brief. Leave Model and Effort on Desk's own unless you have a reason.

    You can follow it on the Board and in the desk's "In hand". The result lands in the desk's "Done" and on your phone.

    Approve or send back a draft

    Nothing is sent, posted, booked or spent without your OK. When a desk wants to do one of those, its pod turns yellow and the draft waits in the In-tray.

    1. Open the In-tray: the In-tray tab (its number is how many are waiting), the yellow pod, or I.
    2. Read the box at the top first. The evaluations desk has already checked the draft: Passed, or what problem is left.
    3. Approve carries out the steps you read, with only the tools they name and the addresses and links in the draft. To change it, type what should change in the box and press Reject; it goes back to the desk with your note.
    If the office refuses (for example the draft changed while you read it), a red line under the buttons says so and nothing is sent.

    On your phone, the same draft arrives in Telegram with APPROVE and REJECT buttons.

    Look after a desk

    Click a desk's card in the executive suite or its pod in the war room (or press 1 to 6 for the department leads). The war room zooms to its team and its page opens on the right.

    1. Work: today's spend against its budget, whether it may send things (with your OK), its model, the tools it uses, and its standing brief.
    2. Brief this desk changes its standing brief. Pause desk stops it taking new work. The box gives a job to this desk only.
    3. Below: its live thoughts, what it has In hand, and what it has Done. Open any ticket to read the result, reply, or retry.
    4. Chat lets you talk to the desk directly.

    Close it, or press 0 or Esc, to go back to the whole office.

    The views

    The tabs along the top. Each one slides in from the right; Esc closes it.

    Numbers N

    Leads and link clicks per day, clicks by channel, where every lead stands, and the paid ads. Switch 7 or 14 days. Every chart has Show as table. Hatched days were not tracked yet; they are not zeros.

    Board B

    Every job in columns: Next, Doing, Waiting for you, Done. Open one to read it, reply to it, retry it or cancel it.

    Ledger L

    What the office has cost on your API key: yesterday, today, by desk, by model and by engine, and every run. Past the daily ceiling ($10 unless you change it), new jobs wait until midnight.

    Calendar C

    Routines ahead, jobs booked for later, and work done, by day. The lane at the top is what runs every day. Press + on a day to book a job for it.

    Routines R

    The office's clock. Add one in plain words (every weekday at 8am send me the inbox digest), run one now, pause, edit or delete it. Workflows on the right are chains of desks you can start with a topic.

    Brain G

    Your notes, the office's memory (a folder of markdown files), as a map or a list. A note lights up when a desk reads it. Tap one to read it and see what it links to.

    What the colours mean

    The ring round each pod, and the word under it, say what the desk is doing.

    Idle

    Nothing to do.

    Working

    Busy, or open in your hand.

    Waiting for you

    A draft needs your OK.

    Failed

    Its last job failed. Open it to retry.

    Paused

    Takes no new work.

    Across the page, green means fine, yellow needs you, orange is a problem, and red failed. Teal only ever means "live" or "you can press this".

    On a small screen and on your phone

    On any screen under 1280 pixels wide the page becomes one page at a time, with tabs at the bottom: War room, In-tray, Numbers, Logs and More (everything else). The + button gives a job. Pausing the office there takes a second tap, so a thumb cannot do it by accident.

    Your phone is Telegram

    The office talks to you through your Telegram bot. Type a job in plain words and it becomes a ticket. Drafts arrive with APPROVE and REJECT buttons, and results land there too.
