# Code of conduct

Nightdesk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people who are new, accept criticism of your work gracefully, and
assume good faith. Harassment, insults, and publishing other people's private details are not
accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

To report a problem privately, use the "Report content" option on the comment, or open a private
security advisory on the repository and say it is about conduct. The maintainer will reply, keep the
report confidential, and act in proportion: a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent one.
