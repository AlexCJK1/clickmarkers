# Code of conduct

Clickmarkers follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people who are new, accept criticism of your work gracefully, and
assume good faith. Harassment, insults, and publishing other people's private details are not
accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

To report a problem privately, email the maintainer at <cojoaca.alex@gmail.com> with "Conduct" in the
subject. The maintainer will reply, keep the report confidential, and act in proportion: a warning, a
temporary ban, or a permanent one.
